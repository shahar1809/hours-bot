Privacy Policy - HoursBot

Last updated: November 4, 2026

HoursBot is a small private tool that lets a limited number of people log their work hours in WhatsApp and receive a monthly summary. It is not offered to the general public.

What we collect

Only what you send to the bot: your WhatsApp phone number, and the messages you write to it (for example a place name and the hours worked).

How we use it

To record your hours, reply to your messages, and send you your monthly report. Nothing else.

Storage and sharing

Data is stored on Netlify's servers and is accessible only to the operator of the bot. We do not sell, share or use your data for advertising or any other purpose.

Retention and deletion

You can delete your last entry at any time by sending "מחק" to the bot. To have all of your data deleted, contact us at the address below and we will remove it.
